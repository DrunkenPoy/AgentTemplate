- Interview the user about ambiguous requirements. Consult records for decisions already made.

- Distinguish completed verification from unperformed or unverified checks.
- Provide handoffs for subsequent agents; reviewers directly inspect the evidence.
- Separate operational records from final office files. Create and verify Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and PDF deliverables with appropriate tools.
- Check actual file and command capabilities. Do not claim to have processed inaccessible files or called unavailable agents.
